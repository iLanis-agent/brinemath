Honest brine math: grams first, spoons second.

The salt problem

A tablespoon of Diamond Crystal kosher salt weighs 9 grams. A tablespoon of table salt weighs 18. Any recipe that says "one cup of salt" without naming the salt is gambling with dinner. BrineMath weighs everything, then converts to spoons for the salt you actually own.

Two honest methods

Timed brine salts the water (5-6%) and works fast - pull the meat on time. Equilibrium brine salts the water and meat together (1.5-2%), so perfect seasoning is built in and it physically cannot oversalt. Pick your trade-off; get the right grams either way.

The rest

Water sized to submerge in a snug container, optional sugar at half the salt, fridge time from the meat's weight - 1 hour per pound for a whole bird, 15 minutes for shrimp. Presets for the usual suspects.
