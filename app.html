The honest part

A tablespoon of Diamond Crystal kosher salt is 9 grams; a tablespoon of table salt is 18. Recipes that say "1 cup of salt" without naming the salt are gambling with your dinner. BrineMath weighs everything in grams first, then spoons second. And brine stays in the fridge the whole time - a brining bird on the counter is a petri dish with good intentions.
